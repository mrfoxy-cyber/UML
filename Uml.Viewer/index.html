A quick guide to the connections
The diagram type sets the visual language. These symbols keep their meaning across views.
╌╌▷ Dependency
The source uses or depends on the target.
──▷ Inheritance
A hollow triangle points to the parent class.
╌╌▷ Implementation
A dashed line and hollow triangle point to an interface.
◇── Aggregation
A hollow diamond marks the whole. The parts can exist independently.
◆── Composition
A filled diamond marks composite ownership.
── Association
A relationship between elements, with no implied ownership.
→ Call · ⇢ Reply
Messages read in order in a sequence; a communication view groups them around collaborators.
alt · Guarded alternatives
Each branch has a condition. Open its frame to inspect the paths.
Named groups organize elements. Their borders do not imply UML ownership. Collapsed groups summarize their original endpoints.
